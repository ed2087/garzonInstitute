Preserving the Heart of the Amazon

Science ❯ Innovation ❯ Community ❯

Join the Mission

Empowering Communities Through Science

We bring scientific innovation to rural and Indigenous communities in the Amazon, protecting ecosystems while empowering people through knowledge and technology.

Our integrated approach combines cutting-edge research with traditional knowledge to create sustainable solutions that preserve biodiversity and improve quality of life.

What We Do

Fossils and cave exploration in the Amazon uncovering ancient biodiversity and climate history to inform conservation efforts.

Water-Y

Atmospheric water tech using nanomaterials to provide clean drinking water in remote regions with limited infrastructure.

Our Team

Doménica Garzón

CEO & Theoretical Physicist

Leading interdisciplinary research at the intersection of physics and ecology.

About Doménica

Dr. Garzón holds a PhD in Theoretical Physics from MIT and has dedicated her career to applying complex systems theory to ecological problems. She founded the Garzón Institute in 2018 after witnessing the impact of climate change on her childhood home in the Amazon.

Her work has been recognized with numerous awards, including the National Science Foundation's CAREER Award and the MacArthur Fellowship.

Carlos Mendez

Director of Field Operations

20 years experience working with Indigenous communities in the Amazon basin.

About Carlos

Born and raised in the Amazon region, Carlos has spent two decades building relationships with Indigenous communities and facilitating knowledge exchange between traditional ecological practices and modern scientific research.

He holds a Master's in Anthropology and is fluent in five Indigenous languages of the Amazon Basin.

Luisa Fernandez

Lead Materials Scientist

Developing sustainable nanomaterials for environmental applications.

About Luisa

Dr. Fernandez is a pioneer in biomimetic nanomaterials, taking inspiration from natural structures found in the Amazon rainforest. Her innovative water filtration technologies are now deployed in over 100 communities worldwide.

She previously led R&D at the Water Innovation Lab before joining the Garzón Institute in 2019.

Rajiv Patel

Community Programs Director

Designing educational programs that bridge traditional knowledge and modern science.

About Rajiv

With a background in both education and environmental science, Rajiv creates innovative learning experiences that honor Indigenous knowledge while introducing scientific methodologies that communities can adapt and control.

His Co-Emprende youth program has launched over 50 sustainable businesses in the Amazon region.
